## Behavior

- Completion records COMPLETED and retains HELD escrow. A shared Lagos event-end + 72-hour deadline controls both disputes and release. Disputes are admitted strictly before the deadline; release is eligible at/after it. Ledger lifecycle locks recheck state, time, reservations and unresolved disputes. The worker completes attendance separately from wallet release. Event staffing treats completed work as finished at event end even while its earnings remain held. Early admin usher-favour resolution restores COMPLETED/HELD; mature resolution may credit the wallet atomically. PAID means a recorded wallet credit. Historical released funds remain protected from new freezes, refunds or duplicate credits; no clawback is introduced.
- Client cancellation reserves one immutable intent containing the accepted time, event start, window and integer-kobo settlement. Refunds are 100%, 50% or 0% at the approved boundaries; the remaining allocation includes 15% commission. Late requests explicitly confirm the displayed window and refund. Retries and delayed approvals retain the original amounts. No processing fee is deducted from the client refund.
- Above ₦50,000, the approval threshold is the gross allocation, including zero-refund cancellations. Two distinct admins must propose/check before dispatch or ledger finalization. Pending approval is excluded from automatic recovery scheduling. Rejection leaves the original reservation intact for review and reproposal. A generic refund cannot replace it.
- Confirmed settlement atomically appends refund, net payout and fee entries, wallet credit and the settlement audit. Zero refund skips provider dispatch. Uncertain outcomes retain held funds; an audit/recording failure retains PROVIDER_OK for recovery without another refund POST. Confirmed provider failure is exposed for support review and never silently reissued.
- API booking details expose the earliest payout time, authoritative dispute eligibility and a safe cancellation summary. Wallet pending previews include frozen earnings and the reserved cancellation split. Mobile distinguishes completed/held, processing, awaiting approval, failed and settled outcomes. Admin has a cancellation queue and review/proposal/checker flow with the original quote and actual refund/net payout/fee.
- Arrival/check-in cannot consume a pending cancellation allocation. No-show sweeping skips competing cancellation intents and continues to other bookings.

## Data and operations

No new schema migration is required. Cancellation snapshots use existing PaymentOperation JSON and existing approval, audit and scheduling fields. Financial writes remain exclusively in `payments/ledger/ledger.ts`; existing append-only entry types and reconciliation principal formula are retained. Original Payment allocations remain historical; cancellation summaries and ledger entries supply actual split settlement amounts.

Deploy API and worker from the same validated revision, followed by admin and mobile. Previously completed/HELD undisputed bookings become eligible after the shared deadline. Historical RELEASED/PAID funds are not moved again. Review legacy inconsistent state manually; do not backfill a second credit or pretend withdrawn funds remain held. The previous release record remains unchanged. Provider account approval, charges, limits and full external certification remain tracked separately under OVA-166/114.
