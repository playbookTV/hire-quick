# HireQuick Paystack live-readiness plan — 26 September 2026

Status: implementation plan, not completed provider accounting or live certification. The owner chose a separate HireQuick Paystack account. Its creation, compliance approval and integration identity have not yet been verified. No provider request was sent and no account settings were changed while preparing this plan.

**Legal/provider correction, 26 September:** Paystack's current [ineligible-business guidance](https://support.paystack.com/en/articles/2127042) explicitly lists escrow services as unsupported. Its [merchant agreement](https://paystack.com/terms) also restricts merchants from acting as payment service providers/payment aggregators. The proposed conditional holding model requires an explicit eligibility assessment; do not assume Manual Payouts activation permits it or that support can waive regulatory requirements. Obtain Nigerian counsel's classification of the actual contracts, custody and control of funds, and retained earnings balances before treating this as an approved architecture. Separately, the Ministry of Labour [licenses recruiters/private employment agencies](https://labour.gov.ng/professional-operations-departments/employment-and-wages/); determine HireQuick's domestic-placement licence or employer-permit requirements. A provider-approved arrangement with an appropriately licensed custodian may be a route, but has not been established for HireQuick.

## 1. Account setup and approval

The account owner completes registered-business onboarding for the dedicated HireQuick integration and submits the [prepared request](paystack-account-confirmation-request.md). Request written acceptance of the exact lifecycle: client payment, attendance verification, event end plus 72 hours, dispute extensions, released withdrawable earnings, refunds and platform transfers. Manual Payouts enablement alone is not evidence of permission for every part of that lifecycle.

Paystack's [Manual Payouts guide](https://support.paystack.com/en/articles/2131074) requires compliance approval and a Nigerian partner Manual Payout Account. Its documented dashboard steps are Settings > Accounts, Sign Consent, Request Single Payout Account, then support activation. Verify the resulting payout schedule reads “Settled to Balance.” Record account-specific holding limits, funding timing, fee terms and the approved transfer security process.

Use new-account TEST credentials and recipient records for acceptance testing. Do not reuse another integration's balances or provider identifiers. Store credentials in the deployment secret manager; record only redacted account/environment evidence here. If Paystack does not approve retained withdrawable balances, obtain its supported settlement proposal before changing the product lifecycle.

## 2. Provider accounting implementation

The current `reconciliation.ts` derives expected available cash from gross escrow movements minus paid withdrawals. The HTTP adapter's `verifyChargeKobo` retains status and amount but not provider fees. These paths do not account for opening funds, external top-ups, net settlements and actual provider charges. Preserve the customer ledger invariants while introducing a separate account-level record of cash movements.

1. Scope every imported record and reconciliation run to provider integration, TEST/LIVE environment and currency. Establish an evidenced opening balance at a known cutoff; never assume even a new account starts at zero.
2. Add an append-only provider movement store. Import all pages of the [balance ledger](https://paystack.com/docs/api/transfer-control/#fetch-balance-ledger), deduplicating by integration/environment/provider entry ID. Keep signed integer-kobo movements, provider timestamp, balance, source type and source ID. Check continuity and surface missing or unclassified movements; a partial import must not report a clean reconciliation.
3. Link provider movements to orders, refunds, withdrawals, platform transfers and settlement batches. Retain verified charge fees and use settlement evidence to distinguish a successful charge awaiting funding from cash actually available. Confirm exact response contracts with the dedicated account; preserve evidence rather than guess from free-text descriptions.
4. Reconcile opening cash plus recorded credits minus debits against the provider balance at a consistent cutoff. Separately reconcile those movements against HireQuick's expected transactions and outstanding customer obligations. Merely matching an imported provider ledger to its own balance cannot detect an unexpected payout. Do not subtract fees twice when already included in a net movement.
5. Model pending transfer cash effects explicitly. Paystack [deducts principal and fee at transfer creation](https://paystack.com/docs/transfers/how-transfers-work/), before final success. Recognize reversals from actual evidence, and do not assume every fee is refunded. Retain existing reference-based retry protections.
6. Record provider costs as HireQuick expenses. Ushers retain their agreed pay and cancellation refunds follow the approved proportional policy. Block platform transfers that would consume customer obligations or the operating reserve. Reserve pending payouts consistently so an already-debited transfer is not counted twice. Recheck availability atomically when scheduling concurrent transfers.

## 3. Funding and acceptance

Finance supplies an operating float for charges, settlement delays and unrecovered fees on full refunds. A platform fee is revenue before provider costs, not entirely withdrawable profit. Size the reserve from confirmed charges and payout/refund exposure; do not hard-code an arbitrary amount or use another client's held funds to cover a shortfall.

Test nonzero collection and transfer fees, delayed net settlement, top-ups, full/partial refunds, pending/failed/reversed transfers, duplicate records, missing pages, unknown movements, currency/account isolation and concurrent platform transfers. Require exact customer entitlements and an explained provider balance after each scenario. Keep live launch pending written account approval and a separately authorized small live acceptance check; TEST results alone cannot certify live settlement timing or actual charges.

Completion evidence: Paystack case and written approval, redacted activation evidence, accepted fee/limit schedule, reviewed migration and accounting implementation, passing automated scenarios, and a reconciled controlled live cycle on the HireQuick account.
