## Design Context

### Users
HireQuick serves event planners, wedding coordinators, corporate organizers, private hosts, freelance ushers, and operations/support/finance staff in Lagos. Clients assemble event crews and track attendance and payments. Ushers find work, manage availability, check in, and withdraw earnings. Administrators review identity submissions, disputes, and money decisions.

### Brand Personality
**Calm and trustworthy** — confirmed by Leslie on 2026-09-16. Language should be plain, reassuring, and precise. Confidence should come from accurate status, readable information, and predictable actions.

### Aesthetic Direction
For mobile and admin, preserve the existing product foundation unless a task requests changes. For the marketing website, Leslie explicitly rejected the emerald/cream editorial direction on 2026-09-23 and supplied Qwick, Wonolo, Shiftsmart, and MIRA Event Support as references, plus the official white HireQuick SVG mark. Use a bold, people-first staffing brand: heavy sans-serif headlines, strong contrast, prominent event photography, direct organiser/worker paths, and the supplied logo. The current marketing iteration uses charcoal, white, and yellow; this is a proposed implementation informed by those references, not a user-specified exact palette. Avoid returning to green, decorative serif headlines, arched photos, or invented lettermark logos.

### Design Principles
1. State money and attendance truthfully: distinguish estimates, held funds, available earnings, and bank withdrawals.
2. Support one-handed mobile use with reachable actions and at least 44×44 touch targets.
3. Show what happened, what happens next, and who needs to act; never present a failed request as a zero balance or empty marketplace.
4. Keep consequential decisions beside their event, person, amount, and evidence.
5. Meet the UXRD accessibility baseline: WCAG 2.1 AA text contrast, Dynamic Type, screen-reader labels and states, reduced motion, and status conveyed by more than color.

Source: user direction above and documentation/03-HireQuick-UXRD.md §§2–5, 12–13. Broader aesthetic changes should be evaluated against this context rather than assumed.
